Rental Duration

Understanding Our Minimum Rental Period

We maintain a 3-month minimum rental period for all strength benches, spin cycles, and treadmills. Here is why this policy protects your fitness goals and delivery overheads:

1. **Habit Building Milestone**: Exercise psychology indicates that establishing permanent neurological habits takes an average of 66 days. A 3-month setup ensures you give your body and mind enough time to discover real fitness results.

2. **Logistics Optimization**: Delivering heavy gym gear requires specialized flatbed vans and two-person technician assembly. Spreading logistics across a 3-month window allows us to waive setup fees for you.

3. **Flexible Cancellation**: After the 3-month minimum period expires, your contract turns into a simple month-to-month subscription. Cancel at any time with a 15-day pickup notice.

Transparent Policies

Frequently Asked Questions About Renting

Everything you need to know about delivery, swaps, maintenance, and converting your rental into full ownership.

How does the 70% Rent-to-Own credit work?

If you decide you love your equipment, 70% of every dollar you have paid in rental fees is directly deducted from the original retail purchase price. You can convert at any time during your active lease.

What happens if a machine needs maintenance?

All repairs, motor adjustments, belt tuning, and parts replacements are 100% free. If our mobile technician cannot resolve the issue within 48 hours, we swap your unit for a fresh one at no charge.

Can I swap my equipment during the rental?

Yes! After your initial 3-month commitment, you can swap equipment categories (e.g. treadmill to spin cycle, or dumbbells to rower) free of charge to match your seasonal training cycles.

How does delivery and setup work?

Our white-glove two-person technical transport team carries the machinery into your designated room, completes full mechanical assembly, tests console connections, and removes all packaging.
